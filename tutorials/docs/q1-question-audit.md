## Review and evidence boundaries

An independent reviewer inspected the real source and executed noncanonical traces against the model, finding three grading false positives plus a contradictory snapshot reveal and incorrect resume highlighting. These were reproduced in failing tests and corrected. This was independent technical source/model review; a fully blinded question-only derivation was not recorded, so it is not claimed as a blinded K2 learner review.

Questions 1, 2 and 4 evaluate semantic outcomes, not the guided button sequence. Choice premises are explicit. Their correctness follows the source's guards and by-value construction, not one fortunate execution. Distractors are the supplied concrete misconceptions; every distractor has its own causal explanation and is exercised by the browser walkthrough.

K6 human evidence remains unexecuted. Suggested rubric for the final explanation: the learner identifies (1) predicate state read under the mutex, (2) construction/move before rejection, (3) separate notification and completed use, and (4) join before owner destruction. Ask the learner to explain a changed schedule with close between notification and resumption. Record their reasoning and revisions separately from selected options; clicking a key is not mastery. The explanation prompt is not automatically graded.
